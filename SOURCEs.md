# Source Register

This file records significant sources used in this research.

Sources are classified by provenance. Multiple publications that reproduce substantially identical material from the same originating source are not treated as independent corroboration.

## Source Classification

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Primary | Original regulatory, corporate, legal, governmental, exchange, or other authoritative record |
| Company-controlled | Material published or controlled by OmniPhi or Algoriphi |
| Company-distributed | Material distributed on behalf of the company through a PR or communications service |
| Derivative / syndicated | Material substantially reproduced from another identifiable source |
| Promotional / partner | Influencer, affiliate, sponsored, or partner material |
| Independent secondary | Reporting or analysis with an identifiable independent origin |
| Automated / reputation | Automated security, reputation, domain, or risk assessment |
| Investigative lead | Material useful for identifying claims or research leads but not sufficient by itself to establish them |

## Independence

A source is not considered independent merely because it appears on a different website.

Where possible, this research traces:

publication → originating material → originating organization or person

If multiple publications derive from the same originating material, they are grouped accordingly.

## Provenance Notes

### PR and Media Distribution

Positive media coverage should be traced to its originating press release or company communication wherever possible.

A wire-distributed press release appearing on multiple news, finance, regional, or aggregator websites remains one originating information source unless evidence demonstrates independent reporting.

### Social Media

Promotional social-media posts should be recorded individually but assessed for common provenance.

Relevant indicators include:

- Identical or near-identical wording
- Identical images or videos
- Identical calls to action
- Common hashtags
- Common links
- Referral or affiliate parameters
- Publication timing
- Partner disclosures
- Common agencies or marketing organizations

### Multilingual Material

Translated or substantially equivalent articles should be investigated for common origin.

A translation appearing on a different website or in a different language is not automatically independent reporting.

## Research Status

This source register is a working document.

Classifications may change when additional evidence establishes a source's actual origin, ownership, relationship, or independence.

Where uncertainty remains, the uncertainty should be recorded rather than resolved by assumption.
