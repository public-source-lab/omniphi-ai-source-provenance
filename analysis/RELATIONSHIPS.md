## OmniPhi / Algoriphi Connection

The existence and nature of any connection between the historical Levitee/Jayconomics matter and OmniPhi / Algoriphi, Inc. requires separate evidence.

This repository should not infer such a connection merely from:

- A person's historical corporate position
- Shared professional networks
- Similar business activities
- Promotional activity
- Search-result associations
- Social-media associations
- Third-party descriptions

A specific connection should be documented only when supported by an identifiable source.

## Evidence Standard

For each proposed relationship, the research should attempt to establish:

- Person or entity A
- Person or entity B
- Nature of relationship
- Date or time period
- Source establishing the relationship
- Whether the source is primary or derivative
- Any relevant uncertainty

## Relationship Status Labels

### Established

Directly supported by a primary or otherwise highly reliable source.

### Supported

Supported by multiple identifiable sources, but not yet established by a primary record.

### Plausible

There are indications of a relationship, but the available evidence is incomplete.

### Investigative Lead

A relationship worth investigating but not currently supported sufficiently to state as fact.

### Not Established

The available evidence does not currently establish the proposed relationship.

## Important Principle

A chain of individually true facts does not necessarily establish a further conclusion.

For example:

A person worked for Company A.

Company A had a relationship with Person B.

Person B later worked with Company C.

These facts do not, by themselves, establish that the person transferred the activities or practices of Company A to Company C.

Any such conclusion requires separate evidence.
