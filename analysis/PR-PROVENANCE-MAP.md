## Independence Test

A publication should be considered potentially independent only if there is evidence that the publisher originated material independently.

Indicators of independent reporting may include:

- Original interviews
- Original reporting
- Independent document review
- Independent testing
- Distinct analysis
- Original photography or reporting
- Clearly different factual framing

Indicators of derivative material include:

- Identical headline
- Identical paragraphs
- Identical quotations
- Identical product descriptions
- Identical calls to action
- Identical contact information
- Identical disclaimer language
- Identical publication structure
- Explicit attribution to ACCESS Newswire
- Explicit attribution to OmniPhi
- Near-simultaneous publication

## Methodological Rule

If ten websites reproduce substantially identical material from the same OmniPhi press release, this repository will record the material as one originating information source with multiple distribution endpoints.

The number of endpoints may be relevant to understanding the scale of distribution.

It is not, by itself, evidence of ten independent confirmations.

## Future Evidence

Each downstream publication should be added only after its provenance has been examined.

Where several publications are demonstrably derived from the same source, they should be grouped rather than presented as independent corroboration.
