# Multilingual and Regional Distribution Map

## Purpose

This document records substantially similar OmniPhi-related material appearing across different regional or language-oriented websites.

The purpose is to determine whether apparently separate publications represent independent reporting, translation, republication, syndication, or another form of coordinated distribution.

The existence of similar articles does not by itself establish common ownership or deliberate manipulation.

## Provenance Questions

The following questions remain open:

1. Which publication appeared first?
2. Was the article supplied directly by SeaPRwire?
3. Did regional sites receive the material from SeaPRwire?
4. Did the sites copy one another?
5. Are the sites independently owned?
6. Do the sites share hosting or technical infrastructure?
7. Do they share analytics identifiers?
8. Do they share advertising or affiliate infrastructure?
9. Are the author biographies reused?
10. Are the publication dates generated automatically?
11. Are the articles translated from a central source?
12. Is there evidence of a common PR or content-distribution intermediary?

## Independence Assessment

The currently identified regional publications should not be counted as independent confirmation of OmniPhi's claims unless independent reporting can be demonstrated.

At present, they are best treated as:

Potentially derivative / syndicated material

rather than independent journalism.

## Important Limitation

This document does not conclude that the listed websites are owned or controlled by the same organization.

It also does not conclude that James Vance is a fictitious identity.

Those are separate factual questions requiring additional evidence.

## Research Method

Future investigation should compare:

- Exact wording
- Sentence order
- Paragraph order
- Images
- Image filenames
- HTML metadata
- Publication timestamps
- Author pages
- Author biographies
- Contact information
- Domain registration information
- Hosting information
- Analytics identifiers
- Advertising identifiers
- Content-management systems
- Internal article IDs
- RSS feeds
- Sitemap timestamps

The objective is to reconstruct the distribution path rather than infer it from appearance alone.

## Current Conclusion

Multiple regional websites currently appear to carry substantially similar OmniPhi promotional material attributed to James Vance and SeaPRwire.

This is evidence of broad distribution.

It is not, by itself, evidence of multiple independent sources.

The precise relationship between the publications remains under investigation.
