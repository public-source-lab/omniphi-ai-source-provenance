## Company Claims Reported in the Release

The release states that OmniPhi launched a beta version of an agentic trading platform.

Among the claims presented are that:

- OmniPhi places an autonomous AI agent at the center of a unified trading workspace.
- The platform can research, develop, backtest, and execute trading strategies.
- Traders define markets and hard risk limits for the agent.
- The agent can operate autonomously within those parameters.
- Strategies are validated against historical data before being used with live capital.
- OmniPhi does not take custody of user funds.
- Execution is routed through official broker APIs.
- A first cohort of early adopters was already trading with Phi at the time of the release.

The release also presents "institutional-grade" capabilities as part of the company's positioning.

These statements are recorded as claims originating from OmniPhi.

They are not treated as independent verification.

## Technical Claims Requiring Independent Verification

The release describes or references:

- Private virtual machines
- Encrypted broker keys
- Broker API connectivity
- Historical strategy validation
- Autonomous execution within user-defined limits
- Live trading by early adopters
- Execution speeds of under 50 milliseconds under certain infrastructure arrangements

The existence and implementation of these capabilities should be investigated separately.

In particular, the meaning and measurement methodology of any latency claim should be established before treating it as independently verified.
