## Product Description

The website describes OmniPhi as an agentic integrated trading environment.

It states that the platform provides:

- AI-assisted market research
- Natural-language strategy development
- Historical backtesting
- Risk-control configuration
- User-approved broker-connected execution

These statements are recorded as descriptions published by the company.

They are not independently verified by this repository.

## Non-Custodial Claim

OmniPhi's Terms of Service state that the service is non-custodial and does not hold or withdraw user funds.

This is a company-controlled statement.

The repository does not independently verify the underlying technical or operational implementation of this claim.

## Trading and Investment Disclaimer

The Terms state that trading involves risk and that OmniPhi provides research, strategy-building, testing, and execution tools rather than personalized investment advice.

This is recorded as a company statement.
