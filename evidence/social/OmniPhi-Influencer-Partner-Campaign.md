# OmniPhi Influencer Campaign — Evidence Record

## Purpose

This document records publicly displayed social-media promotional material concerning OmniPhi.

The purpose of this document is to:

- document observable promotional activity;
- identify recurring campaign language and calls to action;
- identify selected accounts represented in the retained evidence;
- compare similarities between promotional posts;
- distinguish promotional distribution from independent product validation;
- preserve uncertainty where the available evidence does not establish a conclusion.

This document does not determine whether OmniPhi, an agency, an influencer, or another party coordinated the activity unless supported by separate evidence.

---

## Methodology

Search-result volume is not treated as equivalent to independent corroboration.

Multiple social-media posts may represent:

- one campaign distributed to multiple accounts;
- copied or adapted promotional language;
- reposting;
- affiliate or partner marketing;
- PR or agency distribution;
- independently created commentary.

Accordingly:

**Number of posts != number of independent sources**

The investigation therefore attempts to distinguish:

1. individual social-media posts;
2. unique accounts;
3. promotional relationships;
4. independently originated commentary.

A large number of promotional posts should not be presented as a large number of independent confirmations of OmniPhi's product claims.

# Web-Indexed Campaign Footprint

Web searches conducted during the investigation have identified multiple indexed Instagram posts containing OmniPhi promotional material.

These search results should be treated as a **minimum observed footprint**, not a complete census.

Search engines do not provide a reliable census of all Instagram posts using a particular hashtag or campaign phrase.

Search indexing may:

- omit posts;
- surface stale results;
- duplicate results;
- return multiple posts from the same account;
- fail to index private or deleted material;
- expose only fragments of captions;
- provide incomplete account information.

Therefore:

**Indexed post count should not be presented as account count.**

Before an indexed result is added to the account-level evidence register, the account and post should be independently identified where possible.

# Distinguishing Posts From Accounts

The investigation distinguishes the following:

### Search result

A page or result surfaced by a search engine.

### Post

A specific Instagram publication or reel.

### Account

The Instagram account that published the post.

### Promotional source

The person, company, agency, affiliate, or other entity that may have supplied the promotional material.

### Independent source

A source that originated its own assessment rather than reproducing promotional material from the same underlying source.

These categories should not be conflated.

For example:

> Seven indexed Instagram posts do not necessarily represent seven accounts.

Likewise:

> Seven accounts using the same campaign copy do not necessarily represent seven independent assessments of OmniPhi.

# Algorithmic Amplification Hypothesis

The campaign's use of engagement-oriented calls to action may be relevant to a hypothesis that increased engagement was intended to increase visibility.

However, the available evidence does not establish:

- the intent of individual commenters;
- how Instagram's ranking system treated the activity;
- whether the comments materially increased distribution;
- whether participants were instructed to comment;
- whether the activity constituted artificial engagement.

Therefore, this repository does not characterize the activity as "algorithm manipulation" as an established fact.

The defensible observation is:

> Multiple promotional posts used an engagement-oriented BETA call to action and, in some cases, encouraged users to interact with the post before receiving beta access.

Whether this activity was deliberately designed to affect algorithmic distribution remains an open question.
