# OmniPhi Influencer / Partner Campaign

## Purpose

This record documents publicly observable evidence concerning OmniPhi promotional activity on social-media accounts.

The objective is to distinguish:

- Ordinary discussion
- Company-controlled marketing
- Influencer promotion
- Partner relationships
- Affiliate activity
- Lead-generation activity

The existence of promotional activity does not by itself establish misconduct.

## Observable Campaign Language

Multiple social-media posts promoting OmniPhi use substantially similar positioning.

One recurring formulation describes OmniPhi as:

"A PhD-led AI startup just gave traders what Claude Code gave developers..."

Other recurring messaging describes OmniPhi as a system that can:

- Turn plain-English trading ideas into working strategies
- Research trading ideas
- Backtest strategies
- Deploy trading agents
- Allow the trader to remain in control

The repetition of distinctive wording across multiple accounts is relevant to determining whether the posts originated from a common marketing campaign.

## Partner Hashtag

Promotional posts have been observed using:

#omniphipartner

This is significant because the hashtag explicitly suggests a partner relationship.

However, the hashtag alone does not establish:

- The legal identity of the partner
- Whether compensation was paid
- Whether compensation was monetary
- Whether commissions were paid
- The existence of an affiliate arrangement
- The terms of any agreement

Those questions require additional evidence.

## Lead-Generation Call to Action

Some OmniPhi promotional posts use a call to action similar to:

"Comment BETA and I'll DM you the private beta link."

This is materially different from simple brand awareness.

It potentially creates a funnel:

Social-media post

↓

User comment

↓

Private direct message

↓

Beta link

↓

OmniPhi signup

Potentially:

↓

Referral / attribution / affiliate tracking

The actual mechanism has not yet been independently established.

## @daytrading

The public Instagram profile for @daytrading identifies itself as:

"The #1 source for crypto, stocks & tech"

The profile publicly lists:

marketing@algofirst.co

and:

algofirst.co/marketing

This establishes a publicly observable relationship between the @daytrading account and the AlgoFirst marketing domain.

It does not, by itself, establish that AlgoFirst arranged the OmniPhi campaign.

## AlgoFirst

Public company-profile information describes AlgoFirst as a marketing-services/community-building company focused on helping brands and products gain visibility.

One public company database identifies AlgoFirst as based in Colombia.

This is secondary information and should not be treated as a substitute for corporate registration records.

## Evidence Classification

### Established

- OmniPhi promotional posts exist.
- Multiple posts use substantially similar campaign language.
- #omniphipartner appears in OmniPhi promotional material.
- Some posts use a BETA comment-to-DM call to action.
- @daytrading publicly lists an AlgoFirst marketing email/domain.
- AlgoFirst publicly presents itself as a marketing/community-building organization.

### Not Established

The currently documented evidence does not establish:

- Exact compensation paid to influencers
- Commission rates
- Affiliate contracts
- Whether every #omniphipartner post was paid
- Whether OmniPhi directly contracted each influencer
- Whether AlgoFirst managed the OmniPhi campaign
- Whether all participating accounts are controlled by one organization
- Whether engagement is artificial or bot-generated
- Whether any disclosure violated applicable law

## Independence Assessment

Influencer posts containing #omniphipartner should not be counted as independent product validation.

They are evidence of marketing activity.

Where multiple accounts publish substantially identical campaign language, they should initially be treated as potentially coordinated promotional material rather than independent opinions.

## Research Questions

The next investigation should determine:

1. Which accounts posted OmniPhi promotional material?
2. When did each account post?
3. Which accounts used #omniphipartner?
4. Which accounts used the "PhD-led AI startup" language?
5. Which accounts used the BETA CTA?
6. Which accounts link to OmniPhi?
7. Are referral or UTM parameters present?
8. Do links contain affiliate identifiers?
9. Do the posts disclose sponsorship or partnership?
10. Is AlgoFirst involved in the campaign?
11. Is Storyline Pros involved in the campaign?
12. Do the same images/videos appear across accounts?
13. Were posts published within a narrow coordinated time window?

## Methodological Limitation

Similarity of promotional language is evidence of possible campaign coordination, but it is not proof of centralized control.

A marketing agency may legitimately provide standardized copy to multiple independent creators.

The relevant question is whether documentary evidence establishes the relationship.

## Primary Evidence To Preserve

For each discovered post, preserve:

- Account name
- Post URL
- Publication date/time
- Screenshot
- Caption
- Hashtags
- Disclosure
- Link/CTA
- Referral parameters
- Image/video
- Any visible sponsor label

Screenshots should be preserved because social-media posts can later be edited or deleted.

## Current Conclusion

The available evidence supports the existence of a coordinated-looking OmniPhi promotional campaign and a publicly observable marketing relationship between @daytrading and AlgoFirst.

The precise commercial structure of the campaign remains unverified.

No conclusion concerning undisclosed compensation, affiliate payments, artificial engagement, or legal violations should be drawn without additional evidence.
