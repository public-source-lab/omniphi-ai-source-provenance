# Alberta Securities Commission — Floreani / Jayconomics Sanctions

## Source Identification

Source: Alberta Securities Commission

Document: Re Floreani, 2025 ABASC 129

Date: September 26, 2025

Source type: Primary regulatory record

Subject: Sanctions and costs following securities-law findings

## What the Record Establishes

The Alberta Securities Commission previously found that James Domenic Floreani and Jayconomics Inc. contravened section 103.1(2) of the Alberta Securities Act.

The provision requires investor-relations communications to clearly and conspicuously disclose that the communication is made by or on behalf of the issuer or security holder.

The ASC found that certain social-media posts made while the respondents were engaged in investor-relations activities on behalf of four issuers did not provide the required clear and conspicuous disclosure.

The September 26, 2025 decision concerns the resulting sanctions and costs.

## Sanctions

The ASC ordered that the respondents be prohibited from participating in the Alberta capital market in specified capacities for a period of at least two years.

The ASC also ordered the respondents, jointly and severally, to pay:

- An administrative penalty of $30,000
- Costs of $10,185.10

The decision explains that ASC sanctions are intended to be protective and preventative and to deter future capital-market misconduct.

## Levitee-Related Finding

The decision identifies Levitee Labs Inc. as one of the issuers for which the respondents conducted investor-relations activities.

It states that the respondents received approximately $84,000 for miscellaneous online marketing services for Levitee.

The decision also describes the respondents' practice concerning sponsorship disclosures and the placement of some disclosures at the bottom of video descriptions where viewers had to select "Show More" to see them.

## Relevance to This Repository

This record is relevant to the historical diligence review of individuals and companies associated with Levitee Labs.

It does not establish that OmniPhi or Algoriphi, Inc. engaged in the conduct described in this proceeding.

Any connection between this historical regulatory matter and OmniPhi must be established separately.
