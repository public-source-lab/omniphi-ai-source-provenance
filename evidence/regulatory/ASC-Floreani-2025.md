# Alberta Securities Commission — Floreani / Jayconomics

## Source Identification

Source: Alberta Securities Commission

Document: Decision concerning James Domenic Floreani and Jayconomics Inc.

Date: April 24, 2025

Source type: Primary regulatory record

Subject: Investor-relations activity and disclosure requirements

## What the Record Establishes

The Alberta Securities Commission found that James Domenic Floreani and Jayconomics Inc. contravened Alberta securities laws in connection with investor-relations activities conducted on behalf of multiple issuers.

The ASC found that certain social-media posts did not clearly and conspicuously disclose that they were made or disseminated on behalf of the relevant issuers.

The decision states that the respondents admitted that certain posts concerning Levitee were made on Levitee's behalf. The decision also discusses evidence including an Independent Contractor Agreement, invoices, and payments.

## Relevance to This Repository

This record is relevant to research concerning the historical activities and relationships of individuals or entities associated with companies discussed elsewhere in this repository.
