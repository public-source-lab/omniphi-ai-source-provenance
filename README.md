# OmniPhi / omniphi.ai — Source Provenance & Due Diligence

An open-source, evidence-based investigation of **OmniPhi (omniphi.ai) / Algoriphi, Inc.**

This repository examines publicly available evidence concerning the company, its product claims, corporate history, public-relations and marketing activity, social-media promotion, technical claims, and the provenance of information appearing across the web.

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to determine:

- What can be independently verified
- What is reported or claimed by the company
- What material originates from public-relations or promotional distribution
- Which apparently separate publications derive from the same underlying source
- What regulatory and corporate records establish
- What technical claims remain independently unverified
- What questions remain unresolved

This repository does **not** begin with a predetermined conclusion about OmniPhi or Algoriphi, Inc.

The objective is to document the evidence and its provenance.

## Core Research Principle

**Search-result volume is not the same as independent corroboration.**

A single press release can appear on many websites through wire distribution, syndication, translation, aggregation, or republication.

Likewise, a coordinated influencer campaign can produce many apparently independent social-media references while representing a single promotional channel.

This research therefore attempts to identify the originating source before counting corroboration.

## Evidence Hierarchy

The research generally gives greater evidentiary weight to:

1. Regulatory and governmental records
2. Court records and legal filings
3. Corporate and securities-exchange records
4. Independent technical documentation or audits
5. Independently verifiable financial or trading evidence
6. Independent reporting with identifiable provenance
7. Company-controlled statements and documentation
8. PR-distributed and syndicated material
9. Promotional, affiliate, and influencer material
10. Search-result snippets and automated reputation assessments

Lower placement in this list does not mean that a source is false. It means that the source requires greater consideration of provenance and potential conflicts of interest.

## Evidence Status

The repository distinguishes between:

**Established fact**  
Supported by evidence that can be independently examined.

**Company claim**  
A statement originating from OmniPhi, Algoriphi, or their representatives.

**Partially verified**  
Some underlying facts are independently established, but the complete claim is not.

**Derivative**  
Material substantially originating from another identifiable source.

**Open question**  
Insufficient evidence currently exists to reach a conclusion.

**Hypothesis**  
A possible explanation requiring further investigation.

**Unsupported**  
Adequate supporting evidence has not currently been located.

## Important Limitation

The absence of evidence is not treated as evidence of misconduct.

Likewise, the existence of promotional relationships, PR distribution, repeated web material, or similar corporate histories does not by itself establish fraud, deception, or other wrongdoing.

Conclusions should be based on the underlying evidence and its provenance.
